Hi, I'm Gabriel. I recently finished my mathematics degree at the University of Zürich & ETH.

I'm head of AI at synature.ai, where we measure biodiversity through animal vocalizations — essentially, signal processing. Before that, I worked at a few startups, mostly on NLP and cloud data pipelines, improving memory usage and processing speed. I've also worked on regression problems at CERN and statistical portfolio optimization at university.

I created this website to give back some of what I've learned — sharing blog posts, good reads, and things I find interesting.
