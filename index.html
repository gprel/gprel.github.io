Hi, my name is Gabriel. I work as head of artifical intelligence at synature.ai I'm a mathematics student at the University of Zurich and ETH Zurich, currently working as Head of Machine Learning at Synature, where I build real-time bioacoustic ML systems for biodiversity monitoring used across multiple countries and thousands of species. Before that I worked on Higgs boson mass regression with graph neural networks at CERN, built serverless data infrastructure for battery data at Sphere Energy, and worked on retrieval and language models at Janzz.technology.

What draws me to this work is the overlap between rigorous math — probability, statistics, the theory behind why a model should work — and the practical reality of getting something to actually run: fast, at scale, on real hardware. Acoustic signal processing sits right at that intersection, turning raw sound into something a model can learn from, under noisy, imperfect, real-world conditions.

I like problems where a waveform, a probability distribution, and a line of optimized code all describe the same thing.
